Who We Are

More Than a Ring

STERLYN was born from a single belief: that genuine handcraft should never be a luxury reserved for few. We are a small, passionate team of silversmiths and designers based in Sri Lanka — a country with centuries of jewellery tradition running through its veins.

Every STERLYN ring begins as a sheet or wire of pure 925 sterling silver. It is shaped, soldered, hammered, set, and polished entirely by human hands. No shortcuts. No mass production lines. Just artisan hands and a stubborn commitment to pieces that last a lifetime.

“We don't just make rings. We make the rings you'll still be wearing in twenty years.”

How It's Made

The Craft Process

Six steps. All by hand. Every single ring.

Design

Every ring starts as a sketch — refined until it's worthy of silver.

Cut

925 silver sheet or wire is precisely cut to shape by our silversmiths.

Form

Silver is annealed and bent into its final shape using hand tools and mandrels.

Solder

Joints are fused with silver solder using a precision torch — seamlessly strong.

Set

Where applicable, gemstones are hand-set and secured by expert stone setters.

Polish

Each ring is hand-polished to your chosen finish — gleaming and ready to wear.

Every ring is individually inspected before leaving our workshop. If it doesn't meet our standard, we start again.
